About Us

Problem: Students struggle to find genuine internships, while companies face high recruitment costs and unverified applicants. Early-year students are largely excluded from opportunities.

Vision: To become India's most trusted internship ecosystem connecting students, companies, and colleges seamlessly.

Mission: Empower students with real-world experience through verified, AI-matched internships and structured mentorship.

Goals: Onboard 10,000+ students and 500+ companies in Year 1, expand nationally by Year 3, and bridge the gap between academia and industry.

We onboard companies through targeted cold emails and LinkedIn outreach to HR managers and startup founders. Direct visits to tech parks and networking events build personal connections. We collaborate with college placement cells to offer companies pre-vetted, motivated interns — reducing hiring time and cost significantly.
